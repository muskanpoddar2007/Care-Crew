How It Works

From symptom to case sheet in four simple steps.

01

Describe your symptoms

Tell Care Crew what's wrong, in Hindi, English, or Hinglish — just like talking to a nurse.

02

Deterministic, clinically-guided questions — no guessing, no hallucinated diagnoses.

Get a verified case sheet
