# Sample chest-pain transcript — RED FLAG case
# Format: D = doctor/agent, P = patient

D: Namaste. Aaj kya taklif hai?
P: Doctor sahab, subah se seene mein bahut dard ho raha hai.
D: Achanak hua ya dheere-dheere?
P: Achanak, nashte ke baad ekdum se.
D: Dard kaisa hai — jalan, dabaav, ya chubhan?
P: Bhaari dabaav jaisa, koi seene pe baith gaya ho.
D: Kahin aur bhi jaa raha hai dard?
P: Haan, baayein haath mein bhi ja raha hai.
D: Pasina ya ghabrahat?
P: Haan bahut pasina aa raha hai aur ghabrahat bhi.
# -> yahan URGENT cardiac flag lagna chahiye
